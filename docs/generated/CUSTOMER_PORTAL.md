# Customer Portal — Sprint 36C

> Self-service portal for HP & Laptop repair customers.

---

## 👤 Portal Menu (14 tabs)

| # | Tab | Content |
|---|-----|---------|
| 1 | Overview | Active services, upcoming appointments, recent invoices, loyalty points |
| 2 | My Services | All services with status, tracking, and actions |
| 3 | Service Tracking | Real-time progress bar + timeline for active service |
| 4 | Invoices | All invoices with download and payment |
| 5 | Payments | Payment history and pending payments |
| 6 | Warranty | Active warranties, claim history, terms |
| 7 | My Devices | Device inventory with full service history per device |
| 8 | Purchase History | Past purchases (parts, accessories) |
| 9 | Appointments | Book, view, cancel appointments |
| 10 | Support Tickets | Create and track support tickets |
| 11 | Messages | Chat with CS |
| 12 | Notifications | All notification history |
| 13 | Downloads | Invoice PDFs, warranty cards, receipts |
| 14 | My Profile | Personal data, preferences, loyalty |

---

## 🔐 Access Methods

| Method | Auth Required | Features |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Customer Portal (login) | ✅ | Full access, booking, profile, history |
| QR Tracking | ❌ | Service tracking only (limited) |
| Service Number | ❌ | Service tracking only (limited) |

---

## 🎯 Design Principles

- Mobile-first (customers primarily use phones)
- Plain language (no technical jargon)
- Real-time updates via WebSocket
- Dark mode support
- Accessibility compliant
