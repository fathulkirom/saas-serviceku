## Membership Levels

| Level | Criteria | Benefits |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Regular | Default | Standard |
| Silver | 5+ services OR Rp 5M spending | 5% discount |
| Gold | 15+ services OR Rp 15M spending | 10% discount + priority |
| Platinum | 30+ services OR Rp 50M spending | 15% discount + priority + free pickup |
