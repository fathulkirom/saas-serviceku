# POS Sales Workspace

> Complete POS & sales workspace — 12 tabs, 4 sidebar widgets.

---

## Tab List

| Tab | Content |
|------|---------|
| **Overview** | KPI — sales today, transactions, avg basket, open orders, pending delivery, profit, top products, channel breakdown |
| **Items** | Product catalog, barcode/QR scan, search, favorites, quick keys |
| **Payments** | Multi-payment: cash, transfer, QRIS, debit, credit, e-wallet, split, installment |
| **Customer** | Customer lookup, loyalty, membership tier, purchase history |
| **Promotion** | Auto-apply promotions, coupons, vouchers, flash sales |
| **Delivery** | Pickup/courier/internal, tracking, proof of delivery |
| **Invoice** | Generate, print, email, WhatsApp invoice |
| **Returns** | Return, exchange, refund, store credit with approval |
| **Timeline** | Full sales activity timeline |
| **Activity Log** | Complete audit trail |
| **Documents** | Invoices, receipts, delivery notes |
| **History** | Full sales history with search |
