# Service Checklist Guide — Sprint 36A

> Standard intake checklist for HP & Laptop service with 10 categories and 55 items.

---

## 📋 Checklist Categories

### Mandatory Categories (3)

| # | Category | Icon | Items |
|---|----------|------|-------|
| 1 | Body / Casing | 📱 | Goresan, penyok, retak, bagian hilang, warna pudar |
| 2 | LCD / Layar | 🖥️ | Retak LCD, dead pixel, garis, backlight bleed, touch issue, flicker |
| 3 | Charging / Baterai | 🔋 | Port longgar/mati, baterai kembung, cepat habis, mati total, wireless dead |

### Optional Categories (7)

| # | Category | Icon | Items |
|---|----------|------|-------|
| 4 | Touchscreen | 👆 | Dead zone, ghost touch, delay, crack |
| 5 | Kamera | 📷 | Depan/belakang blur/mati, flash mati, lensa tergores |
| 6 | Speaker / Audio | 🔊 | Suara pecah, speaker mati, volume rendah, mic mati/berisik, earpiece |
| 7 | Face ID / Fingerprint | 🔐 | Face ID mati, fingerprint mati/longgar |
| 8 | Keyboard / Trackpad | ⌨️ | Keycap hilang, tombol macet/mati, backlight, trackpad mati/jumpy |
| 9 | WiFi / Bluetooth | 📶 | WiFi mati/lemah, Bluetooth mati/gagal pairing |
| 10 | Mainboard / Komponen | 🔧 | Korosi, korsleting, water damage, bekas perbaikan, komponen hilang |

---

## ✅ Usage Flow

```
CS receives device
  → Open Intake Checklist
  → Check mandatory categories (Body, LCD, Charging)
  → Check optional categories as applicable
  → Take intake photo for each issue
  → Customer signs checklist
  → Checklist stored with service record
```

---

## 📸 Photo Requirements

- Each checked item SHOULD have a corresponding photo
- Photos categorized as `intake`
- Minimum: 1 photo per mandatory category with issues
