## Technician Capabilities

- ✅ View today's jobs with priority
- ✅ Start/pause/resume/finish jobs
- ✅ Upload before/after photos
- ✅ Add diagnosis notes
- ✅ Complete repair checklist
- ✅ Request spare parts (linked to Inventory)
- ✅ Request supervisor approval
- ✅ Capture customer digital signature
- ✅ Track work time
- ✅ View performance stats
