# Customer Reporting

> 5 Enterprise Reports for Customer Analytics.

---

## 📊 Report Catalog

### 1. Customer Growth
| Dimension | Metric |
|-----------|--------|
| Time (daily/weekly/monthly/yearly) | New customers, Churned, Net growth |
| Source channel | Count, conversion rate |

### 2. Customer Segmentation
| Segment | Metrics |
|----------|---------|
| By spending tier | Count, % total, total revenue |
| By service frequency | Count, avg ticket |
| By device type | Count, brand distribution |

### 3. Top Customers
| Filter | Metric |
|--------|--------|
| Top 10/25/50/100 | Total spent, service count, LTV, profit |
| Period | This month, Q, YTD, all time |

### 4. Customer Lifetime Value
| Dimension | Metric |
|-----------|--------|
| Segment | Avg LTV, median LTV, total LTV |
| Acquisition channel | LTV by source |
| Membership level | LTV by tier |

### 5. Inactive Customers
| Filter | Metric |
|--------|--------|
| Days since last visit (30/60/90/180) | Count, lost revenue, reactivation rate |
| Segment | Contacts, responses, reactivated |

---

## 📈 Dashboard Integration

| Widget | Report Source |
|--------|---------------|
| New Customers Today | Customer Growth (daily) |
| Top Customers | Top Customers (top 5) |
| Churn Alert | Inactive Customers (90d) |
| Segment Pie | Customer Segmentation |
