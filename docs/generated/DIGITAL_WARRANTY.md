# Digital Warranty — Sprint 36C

> Customer-facing digital warranty management.

---

## 🛡️ Warranty Types

| Type | Duration | Covers |
|------|----------|--------|
| Garansi Jasa | 30 days | Technician workmanship |
| Garansi Sparepart | 90 days | Replaced parts |

---

## 📱 Customer Warranty View

Customer Portal → Warranty tab shows:
- Active warranties with remaining days
- Warranty terms and conditions
- Claim history
- Claim button (for active warranties)
- Expired warranties (grayed out)

---

## 📞 Claim Process

```
Customer reports issue within warranty period
  → Open Customer Portal → Warranty tab → Claim
  → Fill claim form (description + photos)
  → CS reviews claim
  → Valid → Free repair under warranty
  → Invalid → New service quotation sent
```

---

## ⚠️ Void Conditions

Warranty void if:
- Physical damage after pickup
- Water damage after pickup
- Opened by unauthorized party
- Customer declined recommended repair that caused recurrence
