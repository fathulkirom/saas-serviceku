# Risk Engine

> Enterprise risk management with 5×5 matrix, heatmap, and AI-powered predictive analysis.

---

## 🎯 Risk Register

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Risk Code | Auto-generated (RSK-{YYYY}-{####}) |
| Risk Title | Brief description |
| Category | Operational, Financial, IT, Security, Legal, HR, Supply Chain, Compliance, Strategic, Reputation |
| Likelihood | 1-Rare → 5-Almost Certain |
| Impact | 1-Very Low → 5-Very High |
| Risk Score | Likelihood × Impact (1–25) |
| Risk Level | Low (1–5), Medium (5–10), High (10–15), Critical (15–25) |
| Mitigation | None, Planned, In Progress, Completed |
| Review Date | Next scheduled review |

---

## 📐 Risk Assessment

- **Qualitative**: Expert judgment with 5×5 matrix
- **Quantitative**: Monte Carlo simulation, Value at Risk (VaR)
- **Residual Risk**: After mitigation controls applied
- **Risk Appetite**: Configurable per category and business unit

---

## 🔥 Risk Heatmap

Visual heatmap rendering risks by likelihood × impact with:
- Color coding (green → yellow → orange → red)
- Bubble size proportional to exposure value
- Drill-down by category, owner, department
- Trend analysis (risk score change over time)
