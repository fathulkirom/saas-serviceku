# Membership Engine

> Enterprise Loyalty & Membership System integrated into CRM.

---

## 🔄 Level Definitions

| Level | LTV Threshold | Service Count | Benefits |
|-------|---------------|---------------|----------|
| **Regular** | Rp 0 – 5M | 0–4 | Standard treatment |
| **Silver** | Rp 5M – 15M | 5–14 | 5% discount, priority queue |
| **Gold** | Rp 15M – 50M | 15–29 | 10% discount, priority queue, free diagnostic |
| **Platinum** | Rp 50M+ | 30+ | 15% discount, priority queue, free diagnostic, free pickup/delivery |

## 🤖 Automation Integration

| Trigger | Action |
|---------|--------|
| Customer reaches Silver | Welcome notification |
| Customer reaches Gold | WhatsApp + email congratulation |
| Customer reaches Platinum | Personal call task + WhatsApp |
| Customer drops tier | Win-back campaign trigger |
| Birthday | Special discount coupon |
